## What it is

PlanIt plans trips across flights, hotels and activities. It has no backend: the app follows an MV architecture and persists to LocalStorage, so the interesting work is in the data model and in the design.

It won ESMAD Best Project 2024/25.

## My part

- **Model layer.** Core data logic, the seeded datasets (airports, hotels, activities, flights) and `ModelHelpers.js`.
- **Gamification design.** A system built on the HEXAD player-type framework and Werbach's 6D methodology, backed by five researched personas.
- **UX research and design deliverables.** The competitor moodboard, wireframes and the brand identity (logo and color system).
