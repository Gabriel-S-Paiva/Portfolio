On 20 November 2025 I spent a day at Celfocus's Porto office for their Insider open day. I went with other students from my degree and from other institutions, and the day was about two things: meeting people who work in tech, and seeing what that work looks like up close.

## The talk

The main highlight was a talk called *What no one told you about working in tech*, where professionals shared realistic insights about the industry and about their own career journeys.

That's the kind of session I'd like to see more of. As a student, you hear about the industry through courses and job ads, and hearing it from people doing the job is a different thing.

## Job shadowing

The rest of the day was job shadowing. I spent it with engineers who explained a lot about what software engineering looks like day to day.

That's all I'll say about the specifics. A conversation with someone doing the job tells you things a description of the job can't.

## What I took from it

Honestly, nothing surprised me. I went in with an idea of what the work would be like, and what I saw matched it. I think that's worth saying, because it's easy to write these posts as if every open day is a revelation.

What I got was confirmation, and the chance to put faces to a job I'd only seen described.

## Why I'd do it again

Open days like this are a cheap way to test your picture of a job against reality. If you're a student, go to the ones you can get into, and ask the engineers what they'd tell their past selves.

Thanks to the Celfocus team for hosting the day.
