A 2D browser game about recycling for the Computer Graphics course, in two phases: a platformer where you collect waste, then a camera phase where you sort it into recycling bins with hand gestures.

- Built the whole platformer phase on a plain 2D canvas: the game loop, Euler physics, AABB collision with solid, one-way and moving platforms, pushable boxes, a dead-zone camera and the level.
- The hand-gesture phase and the menu pages were built by my teammate.
