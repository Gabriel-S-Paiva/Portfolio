A social platform pairing the UN's 17 Sustainability Development Goals with a personal bucket-list format: users build activity lists tied to a goal, complete items with photo proof, and earn per-goal badges. Built in a single day at the TeamGreen4All sustainability hackathon.

- Designed the full data model: ER diagram and SQLite schema, including a self-referencing follow relationship and the entity structure behind badges, lists, and activity completions.
- Prototyped the backend structure (MVC layout, not wired to the database in the hackathon window).
- Team built a working frontend from templates to demonstrate the concept live.
